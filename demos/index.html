lightfall — lightcone demos

The charged-blackholes lightcone surface demos, ported to the lightfall engine and drawn in the simple render style for side-by-side evaluation.

  • Near-flat control
    Nearly massless hole (m = 0.02) — the near-Minkowski baseline.
  • Single hole
    The classic single lensed light cone (m = 1).
  • Binary
    Two equal holes side by side (m = 1, 1).
  • Triple
    Three holes, an asymmetric lens (m = 1, 1, 2).
  • Quad
    Four holes (m = 1, 1, 2, 4).
  • Double cone (future + past)
    The full light cone of an event — gold rises, orange falls.
